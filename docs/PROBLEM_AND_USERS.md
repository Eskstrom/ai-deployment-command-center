# Problem and Users

## Problem

Enterprise AI launches frequently stall between contract signature and sustained use. Readiness evidence lives in scattered spreadsheets, infrastructure blockers are discovered late, and product teams cannot tell whether weak adoption is caused by integration, training, workflow fit, or support gaps.

AI Deployment Command Center creates one operational view of rollout readiness, launch risk, and post-launch adoption.

## Primary user: forward-deployed implementation manager

Owns the launch across multiple customer sites and must turn incomplete customer context into a practical deployment plan.

## Secondary users

- Customer champion: completes readiness tasks and sees ownership.
- Product operations lead: identifies recurring rollout friction across accounts.
- Support lead: monitors unresolved launch issues and handoff risk.

## Core decision

The product answers: “Which site needs intervention now, why, and who owns the next action?”

## Out of scope

- Real patient, customer, or clinical data.
- Automated production deployment execution.
- Predictive risk claims without transparent, editable rules.
