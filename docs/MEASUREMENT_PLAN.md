# Measurement Plan

## Core metrics

| Metric | What it measures | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Readiness completion rate | Required launch tasks completed | Launch feasibility |
| Time to unblock | Time from identified blocker to resolution | Delivery efficiency |
| Time to first value | Time from launch to first sustained usage | Customer adoption |
| Adoption by role | Usage across intended user groups | Workflow fit and training quality |
| Issue aging | Unresolved issues over time | Launch and support risk |

## Operating cadence

Review sites weekly before launch and monthly after launch. Every red-risk site needs a named owner, recovery action, and next review date.

## Learning loop

Classify recurring blockers as infrastructure, integration, training, workflow mismatch, product gap, or customer ownership. Product operations reviews the themes each month to determine whether the fix belongs in onboarding, product, documentation, or account planning.

## Next milestone

Build the fictional-site dashboard with editable risk rules and a site detail page showing evidence, next action, and adoption trend.
