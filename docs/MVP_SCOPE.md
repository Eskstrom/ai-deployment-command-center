# MVP Scope

## In scope

- A portfolio view of ten fictional customer sites.
- Readiness checks across infrastructure, integration, training, governance, and accountable owner.
- Site launch status, open issues, issue aging, and adoption trend.
- Transparent risk rules with a visible explanation for every risk level.
- A next-action queue with owner and due date.

## Deliberately deferred

- Integrations with CRM, ticketing, or implementation-management systems.
- Automated outreach or status changes.
- Machine-learned risk scores.
- Customer billing, commercial forecasting, or medical workflow data.

## MVP acceptance criteria

1. A deployment manager can identify blocked sites in under one minute.
2. Every risk status names the contributing conditions.
3. A user can trace a recommended next action to an owner and underlying evidence.
4. The dashboard distinguishes pre-launch readiness from post-launch adoption.
